♠ Our story

Seven years all in

All In Fitness opened in Blessington in September 2019 in a single 2,250 sq ft unit. By Christmas we'd knocked through to next door — and we've kept growing since.

We're a member-focused gym. We train local GAA and soccer teams, run classes every day, and know the people who walk through the door.

♦ Questions

Good to know

Where is All In Fitness?

Unit 2, Burgage House, Burgage, Blessington, Co. Wicklow, W91 V065. Call us on 045 900 372.

How much is a gym membership?

€59 a month (plus a €20 joining fee, with a 3-month minimum), €195 for 3 months, or €525 for a year. Student and OAP memberships are €169 for 3 months or €425 for a year. Every membership includes the full gym and all classes.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. One-to-one personal training is run by the trainers who work at the gym, so call us or ask at the desk and we'll put you in touch. The gym also runs small group training sessions.

What classes do you run?

All In Strength, All In Conditioning, All In Hybrid, HYROX, Men's Boxing and Ladies' Boxing. Class times change through the year, so call us or ask at the desk for what's on.

Do you train sports teams?

Yes. We run strength and conditioning for local GAA and soccer teams.

How do I join?

Buy a membership online through Glofox, or call in to the gym. You register on Glofox, which is also where you fill in the gym's forms and book.
